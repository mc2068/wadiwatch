# WadiWatch: 2-minute video pitch script

Target length: about 1 min 50 s (about 275 spoken words). Voice-over only. Time a read-through before recording; cut the cost sentence first if it runs long.

Rules from the brief, to check before export:

- No faces. No school building, logo, uniform or badge. No names. No mention of the Student Branch.
- Order: Problem -> Solution and technical approach -> Community impact.
- Must state: project name, mission, vision, values.
- Maximum 2 minutes (1 point is lost above that).

| Time | Voice-over | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 - 0:18 | "Nabeul, the twenty-second of September, 2018. Two hundred millimetres of rain fall in a few hours. The wadis fill, streets go under more than a metre and a half of water, and at least five people lose their lives. A weather warning had been issued the day before. It never reached the people standing next to the water." | Black screen with date, then rain footage or animated rain over a simple map of Cap Bon. | 0:18 - 0:30 | "In a flash flood you have minutes, and power and phone networks can fail. So the warning has to be local, automatic, and able to work alone." | Animation: a wadi line filling; icons of a power pylon and phone mast fading out. |
| 0:30 - 0:38 | "This is WadiWatch. Our mission: give every household beside a wadi enough warning to get out of the water's way." | Title card: "WadiWatch" and the mission sentence. |
| 0:38 - 1:05 | "A solar-powered node sits above the wadi. It measures the water level and the rain, and decides the alert level by itself. Over long-range LoRa radio it triggers a siren and a flashing light in the neighbourhood directly: no internet, no mobile network, no mains power. At the same moment a gateway sends SMS to registered residents and farmers, and a dashboard shows the municipality and Civil Protection which wadi is rising and how fast." | Close-ups of hands assembling the node (hands only). The architecture diagram (Figure 2) builds up path by path: red path first, then dashed blue. |
| 1:05 - 1:20 | "It is built from low-cost parts: about three hundred and eighty dinars for a sensing node. It runs for weeks without sun, and a local technician can swap any module with hand tools." | Bill-of-materials figure; the prototype on a bench; a battery module being unplugged and replaced. |
| 1:20 - 1:38 | "The community owns it. A wadi committee of residents, the municipality and Civil Protection sets the thresholds and places the sirens. The five values appear one by one. |
| 1:38 - 1:50 | "WadiWatch advances Sustainable Development Goals eleven and thirteen. Our vision: a Tunisia where nobody dies in a flash flood for lack of a warning." | SDG 11 and 13 tiles; closing card with "WadiWatch" and the vision sentence. |

## Production notes

- Record the voice in one quiet take on a phone held close; add light background music at low volume.
- Reuse the diagrams in `report/figures/`; they match the report.
- Any filmed street or wadi footage must not show people's faces or anything that identifies the school.
- Subtitles in English help the 2 points for editing and make the video usable without sound.
- Figures quoted (200 mm, 1.7 m, at least five deaths, warning issued the day before) are sourced in the report's references [1] and [2].
